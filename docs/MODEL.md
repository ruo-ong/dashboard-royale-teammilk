## 3. The decision

The chosen model (day-of-week average) forecasts a July weekend at $4.88M wagered ($462k expected win). We fund one promotion weekend, Jul 18-20, and keep Jul 25-27 without a promotion as a same-month comparison. At the uplift our own promotions achieved, it should add about $54k of expected win ($14k-$99k) for $15k. Can a lift be detected? On the 7 ordinary weekends of May-June, the model's weekend forecasts were off by -5.5% on average (normal range -10% to -1%), so a lift like our tournament's (+18%) would stand out, but one like Locals Week's (+6%) could not be told apart from forecast error. The model also changes how success is judged: against a flat daily average, an ordinary weekend already looks +12% better, so the simple baseline would declare almost any promotion a success. The day-of-week forecast is the fair yardstick.
