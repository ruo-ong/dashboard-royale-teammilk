The original-project MIT license does not apply to third-party source tables.
Do not publish the complete repository while blocked source tables remain.
Do not treat downloading the original files as a conversion recipe: it does
not reconstruct the normalized tables. A link-only release needs a tested
converter, a new manifest, and corresponding guide/dictionary changes.

Record any obtained permission as a local evidence document and set its path
in `release.json`. Evidence must identify the covered files, the publisher,
the permission date and allowed redistribution terms.
